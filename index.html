Inspired by the reference — a generative recreation, not a pixel trace. Shuffle gives a whole new composition; the sliders and palettes restyle the current one live. Density, Style and Shape are all independent — mix any combination. Simple/Complex resets Targets/Layers/Nodes per layer; Structured/Organic controls scatter; Radial arranges layers as rings collapsing into a central nucleus (targets scattered on the outer ring). Nucleus offset actually moves the nucleus off-center — the outer ring stays put, every layer in between drifts proportionally, and connections skew naturally as a result.
